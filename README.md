# Meticulous Mermaid Cleaning Co

Build an ultra-premium, visually stunning luxury website for **Meticulous Mermaids Cleaning LLC**, based on the uploaded banner and branding.

**Business Information**
- Business Name: Meticulous Mermaids Cleaning LLC
- Phone: 321-258-9631
- Service Area: Brevard County
- Status: Licensed & Insured
- Tagline: “The Best Clean on the Coast!”
- Brand Message: “A Cleaner Home, A Happier You”
- Services: Residential Cleaning, Commercial Cleaning, Airbnb / STR, Move-In / Move-Out, Deep Cleans, Recurring Services.

**Brand Identity**
Use a sophisticated coastal luxury palette inspired by the banner: rich turquoise, ocean teal, deep blue, pearl white, soft coral pink and elegant champagne-gold accents. Incorporate subtle mermaid-inspired details, ocean textures, seashell-inspired shapes and refined coastal elements without making the website look cartoonish.

**Design Direction**
Create an exceptionally premium, feminine, elegant and expensive-looking website. The hero/banner must be extraordinary and custom-designed, featuring a unique editorial layout, beautiful professional cleaning and luxury-home imagery, sophisticated typography, subtle coastal visual effects and premium animations. Every section must have its own distinctive layout and visual identity. Avoid generic cleaning website templates.

**Required Sections**
1. Extraordinary Luxury Hero / Banner with strong headline and booking CTA.
2. About Meticulous Mermaids.
3. Premium Cleaning Services.
4. Why Choose Us.
5. Our Cleaning Process.
6. Before & After Showcase.
7. **Interactive Image Gallery** using my supplied image-gallery component.
8. **Reels Section** with exactly 3 empty, elegant vertical video placeholders (9:16), ready for my own videos.
9. **3D Testimonials** using my supplied 3d-testimonails component.
10. **Interactive Map Section** showing the Brevard County service area. This section is mandatory.
11. Contact & Booking with clickable phone number.
12. Premium Footer.

**Integrate My Two Supplied React Components**

1. `image-gallery.tsx`
   - Integrate the supplied expandable image gallery.
   - Use premium residential cleaning, spotless interiors and coastal luxury-home images.
   - Preserve the expandable hover interaction and responsive layout.
   - Customize its appearance to match the brand.

2. `3d-testimonails.tsx`
   - Integrate the supplied animated 3D vertical marquee testimonial component.
   - Use the supplied Card and Avatar components and required dependencies.
   - Customize the cards, colors and animations to match the coastal luxury theme.
   - Do not use demo names or fabricated customer reviews. Use editable testimonial placeholders until genuine reviews are provided.

Follow the supplied component code and integrate everything inside `/components/ui`. Install missing dependencies, configure Tailwind animations and fix all imports, TypeScript errors and compatibility issues. Maintain the existing shadcn, Tailwind CSS and TypeScript structure.

**Important Requirements**
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Exactly 3 Reels placeholders, no extras.
- Never omit the interactive Map section.
- Use beautiful, realistic, high-quality cleaning and luxury interior photography.
- Add smooth scroll reveals, elegant hover interactions and refined animations.
- Make all business details, images and content easy to edit.
- Do not invent an email, street address, customer reviews, ratings, prices or business claims.
- Keep the design feminine, sophisticated, premium and professionally branded.
- Avoid unnecessary sections, repetitive layouts and generic filler content.

**Final Goal:** Create a distinctive, world-class luxury cleaning website that makes Meticulous Mermaids Cleaning LLC look like a premium coastal cleaning brand. The hero and every section should feel custom-designed, memorable and exceptionally polished.
